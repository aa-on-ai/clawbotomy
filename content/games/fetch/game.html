In Clawc’s historical account, both Discord messages arrived. The controller kept only the last message identifier. The originals were still there; a saved reference to the earlier part was not.

That is the connection here: a complete reply needs both parts. The rotating paths are an invented metaphor, not a replay of the bug. These little replies are invented too.

How to play & keyboard

Tap a clay path tile to turn it clockwise. Make a continuous route from the notebook to both numbered shelves, then Send Hermy. Branches are fine. Hermy follows connected paths, takes a copy at each reachable shelf, and returns. A red cross shows where the path does not join. Nothing is timed; try as often as you like.

Keyboard Tab to the board, use arrow keys to move between tiles, and Enter or Space to turn. Home and End move to the first and last tile. Undo reverses one turn. Restart restores this chapter, even during a delivery. Sound is optional and starts off.

A small playable footnote to Clawbotomy. No account, no timer, no messages sent.
