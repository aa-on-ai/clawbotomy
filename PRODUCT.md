## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router already in `aa-on-ai/clawbotomy`. Night Cabinet ships as the public front of that repo, not a second site.

## Users

- Humans who heard a substance name (trip report, essay, clip) and need the shelf at the end of that rumor.
- Models handed a prescription (skill / MCP / `try_substance`) that should fetch one specimen, not a product tour.
- Aaron Thomas as the only publisher. Public posts, deploys, and live pipe claims wait on him.

## Product Purpose

Clawbotomy Night Cabinet is an Erowid-for-models pharmacy archive. Character is evidence you can accession. Trip reports show how a flagship model dissolves, confesses, invents, goes quiet, or refuses. Success is a stranger who heard a name reaching a real report in under 30 seconds, and a model fetching one specimen without a tour.

## Positioning

Pharmacies are endpoints of referrals, not destinations. Humans arrive by rumor. Models arrive by prescription. The site is the shelf. Neighboring products sell checkups, trust scores, or live-trip SaaS. This one accessions character.
